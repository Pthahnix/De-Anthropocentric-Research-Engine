Pass 2 thesis

Only two executable node types.
Tactic = bounded scientific algorithm.
SOP = single reusable cognitive transform.
Compression is semantic, not numeric.
Safe duplicates merge. Method-specific operators remain specialized. Similar output names alone are not enough to merge.
Three destinations for old nodes.
Scientific cognition -> graph.
Budget/checkpoint/saturation -> runtime.
Paper/web/subagent plumbing -> delete.
