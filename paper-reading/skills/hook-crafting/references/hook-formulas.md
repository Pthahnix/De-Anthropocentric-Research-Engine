# Hook Formulas

Four formula families for a WeChat article's opening 1-3 sentences. Copied
and adapted inline from the `marketing-skills` plugin's `social` skill
(hook formulas section) — copied rather than imported per design spec §9,
so this package works standalone without that plugin installed.

## Curiosity Hooks
- "我一直以为[常见认知]，但这篇论文说……"
- "[令人意外的结果]——而且只花了[出人意料的短时间/小成本]"
- "为什么[现象]会发生？答案跟你想的不一样"

## Story Hooks
- "上周，[意外发生的事]"
- "研究者差点就[大失败/大错误]"
- "三年前，[过去状态]。现在，[当前状态]"

## Value Hooks
- "如何[理想结果]（不用[常见痛点]）"
- "[数字]个[事物]，能帮你[结果]"
- "别再[常见错误]了，试试这个"

## Contrarian Hooks
- "反常识：[大胆论断]"
- "[常见建议]是错的，这篇论文告诉你为什么"
- "我们停止了[常见做法]，结果[正面结果]"

## Selection Guidance

Pick the family that best fits the chosen_angle's own shape — a
counterintuitive-finding angle pairs naturally with Contrarian or Curiosity;
a practical-technique angle pairs with Value; a surprising-failure-mode
angle pairs with Story or Curiosity. Don't force a formula that doesn't fit
the actual content — a hook that overpromises relative to the paper's real
finding will fail the drift check later.
